CarLink 0.4.6 — CarPlay Audio now; CarPlay Video when approved

Bundle ID: com.carlink.CarLink

CURRENT STATE (ships to TestFlight today)
  CarLink.entitlements requests ONLY:
    com.apple.developer.carplay-audio = true
  So the app signs and builds with your current CarPlay Audio capability and
  the existing App Store provisioning profile. Codemagic no longer fails on a
  missing video entitlement — it just reports which CarPlay capabilities the
  profile has.

WHEN APPLE APPROVES CARPLAY VIDEO FOR THIS BUNDLE ID
  1. In Apple Developer, enable the CarPlay Video capability on the App ID.
  2. Regenerate the App Store distribution provisioning profile (an older
     audio-only profile does NOT contain the new entitlement and cannot sign
     a binary that requests it).
  3. Add this key back to CarLink/CarLink.entitlements:
       <key>com.apple.developer.carplay-video</key>
       <true/>
  4. Push to GitHub and start a new Codemagic build.

NOTES
  - The video-in-car flow uses AVPlayer + AirPlay with CPPlaybackConfiguration
    (CarPlay Video, iOS 27) and only offers video when the vehicle reports
    supportsVideoPlayback and is parked.
  - CarPlay shows only Apple's templates (grid, list, Now Playing). It cannot
    render a web browser or arbitrary web video on the car screen. The full
    browser runs on the iPhone; video reaches the car screen via AirPlay.
